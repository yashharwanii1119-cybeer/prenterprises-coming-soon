How can I request a solar installation consultation in Jodhpur? +

What information is needed for a solar installation estimate? +

To prepare an accurate assessment, we typically evaluate your recent electricity bills (to determine monthly kilowatt-hour consumption), your sanctioned power load, and the available shadow-free rooftop area.

What factors affect the cost of a rooftop solar system? +

Rooftop solar costs depend on the required system capacity (kW size), solar panel and inverter specifications, mounting structure elevation, wiring requirements, and site conditions. We provide a transparent itemized quotation following an on-site evaluation.
